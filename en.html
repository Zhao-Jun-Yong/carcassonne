🌱 Beginner's Guide First time? Start here ▾
Each Turn in Three Steps
  • ① Draw & Place — Draw a tile and place it next to existing tiles. All touching edges must match terrain.
  • ② Deploy (optional) — Place one meeple on a feature of the tile you just placed, if it's unoccupied.
  • ③ Score — If a feature was completed, score it immediately and return your meeple.
Common Beginner Mistakes
  • Running out of meeples? Normal — timing your placements to get them back is the strategy.
  • Farmers never come back? Correct. Farmers (laid flat) stay all game and only score at the end.
  • How do shields score? Completed city: +2 pts each. Incomplete at game end: +1 pt each.
  • Can I take over someone's feature? No. But merged features give full score to whoever has the most meeples; ties share.
  • When does a monastery complete? When all 8 surrounding spaces are filled — 9 pts total.
Meeple Role Names
  • On a road → Thief
  • In a city → Knight
  • On a monastery → Monk
  • Lying flat on a field → Farmer

Role names appear in expansion rules — Crop Circles and the Dragon's menu both reference them.

⚖ Referee's Handbook Settle arguments here ▾
Gold Mines
Q: If a gold mine tile completes a feature on the same turn, can gold be claimed immediately?
Yes. Order: ① Place tile → ② Place gold → ③ Score and distribute gold. Gold is placed before scoring.
Q: Can farmers claim gold nuggets?
No. Farmers cannot receive gold at any point — during the game or at final scoring.
Inns & Cathedrals
Q: Can I place a cathedral in someone else's city?
Yes. Legal sabotage — if they fail to complete it, they score 0. Same applies to Inns on opponent roads.
Q: If a road or city with an inn/cathedral is incomplete at game end, do they still score?
No — 0 pts. Worse than a normal incomplete feature, which at least scores 1 pt per tile.
Q: Does the Big Meeple count as two meeples for majority?
Yes — counts as 2 meeples when determining majority.
Traders & Builders
Q: Does the Dragon eat the Builder and the Pig?
Yes. Both are on the Dragon's menu in the Big Box rulebook. Returned to their owner when eaten.
Q: If the Builder is eaten, can the extra turn still be taken?
No. The Builder is no longer on the board, so the condition is not met.
Q: If the Pig is eaten, how do farmers score?
Back to 3 pts per city. The Pig's bonus is lost.
Q: If a Crop Circle tile is drawn during an extra turn, when does the effect trigger?
Complete the entire extra turn first (place tile, deploy meeple, score), then execute the Crop Circle effect.
The Princess & The Dragon
Q: Does the Fairy's 1-pt bonus apply at the start of a Builder extra turn?
No. The Fairy's 1 pt only triggers at the start of a normal turn.
Q: Can the Fairy protect meeples from the Princess tile?
No. The Fairy only protects against the Dragon. Displaced knights cannot receive the Fairy's 3-pt bonus either.
Q: Can the Magic Gate deploy a meeple to an already-occupied feature?
No. Magic Gate follows normal deployment rules — cannot target occupied or completed features.
Q: Can the Dragon eat farmers in fields?
Yes. The Dragon eats all meeples and special figures on any tile it passes through, including farmers.
Q: If the Dragon enters a tile whose feature was already scored but meeples remain, are they eaten?
Yes. The Dragon does not distinguish completed from incomplete features.
Mage & Witch × Princess & Dragon
Q: Does the Dragon eat the Mage or the Witch?
Yes. Both are on the Dragon's menu per the official FAQ. Returned to the supply when eaten.
Q: After placing a magic tile, the Mage and Witch end up on the same feature — what happens?
The player who placed the tile must immediately move one of them, before scoring.
Q: If there are no meeples on the feature when it scores, do the Mage/Witch effects still apply?
Yes. Mage still adds 1 pt per tile; Witch still halves the total (round up). They cannot be on the same feature simultaneously.
Crop Circles
Q: Effect B forces a recall — what if a player has no matching meeple on the board?
They are skipped.
Q: Can a player choose not to deploy under Effect A?
Yes. Effect A says "may" — it is optional.
Q: Under Effect A, can a meeple be placed on a feature occupied by an opponent?
Yes, but only next to your own meeple already there. Follows normal shared-ownership rules.
Ferries
Q: If a Builder's road is extended solely because a ferry moved, does that trigger an extra turn?
No. Extra turns only trigger when a player actually places a new tile.
Q: If a ferry move closes both ends of a road, does it score immediately?
Yes — score immediately. Any event that closes both ends of a road triggers immediate scoring.
Robbers
Q: If a Robber moves due to theft and lands on another scoring marker, does it steal again?
No. No chain robberies — the Robber stops after one theft per trigger.
Q: If multiple Robbers are on the same space, do they all steal?
Yes — each triggers independently. Each Robber's owner receives half the victim's score (rounded up).
Q: Can a Robber steal points triggered by a Messenger card?
Yes. Any scoring action that moves a scoring marker can be stolen from.
Bridges, Castles & Bazaars
Q: How much does a Castle score for a farmer?
4 pts per Castle (vs. 3 pts for a normal completed city).
Q: Can the Dragon enter a tile occupied by a Castle?
No. Meeples in Castles are protected, equivalent to the Count's city.
Q: Can bazaar bidding push a score below 0?
Yes. Negative scores are possible and valid.
Flying Machines
Q: Can a meeple land on an incomplete feature already occupied by an opponent?
Yes. The Flying Machine can land on any incomplete road, city, or monastery regardless of occupants. Fields and completed features are prohibited.
Q: What if the dice result points to a space with no tile?
The meeple is returned. No meeple or special figure may be deployed that turn.
Messengers
Q: If multiple features are scored in one turn, can different scoring figures be moved for each?
Yes. Each separate scoring event can use a different figure; each individual event must use a single figure.
Q: Can Messenger cards chain indefinitely?
Yes — unlimited chains. Each time a scoring figure lands on a dark space, another card may be drawn.
📜 Base Game Core rules ▾
Overview

2–6 players, 72 land tiles, 7 meeples each. One starting tile placed face-up to begin.

Three Steps Per Turn

① Place a Tile

Draw 1 tile and place it adjacent to existing tiles. All touching edges must match. If it cannot be placed, discard and draw again.

② Deploy a Meeple (optional)

Place 1 meeple on a feature of the tile you just placed. You may not deploy to a feature already occupied by any meeple (including your own).

③ Score

If the tile completed any feature, score it immediately and return the meeple(s).

Scoring
During the Game (completed features)
Road (per tile)1 pt
City (per tile)2 pts
Shield in city+2 pts each
Monastery (all 8 surrounding filled)9 pts
End of Game (incomplete features)
Incomplete road (per tile)1 pt
Incomplete city (per tile)1 pt
Shield in incomplete city+1 pt each
Incomplete monastery (itself + surroundings)1 pt each
Farmer (per adjacent completed city)3 pts
Shared Ownership

If a feature is shared, the player with the most meeples scores all points. Ties: everyone with the most scores in full.

Farmers

Farmers lie flat on fields and never return during the game. They score only at game end: 3 pts per completed city adjacent to their field.

Shared field rules apply — the player(s) with the most farmers in a field score all points from that field.
Scoreboard

When reaching or passing 50/100, flip your scoring token or take a 50/100 point tile to track your total.

🌊 The River Mini expansion ▾
Contents

12 river tiles (darker backs), including the Spring and Lake tiles. These replace the starting tile.

Setup

Find the Spring and Lake tiles. Shuffle the remaining 10 river tiles face-down, place Spring on top and Lake at the bottom.

Play

The player who places the Spring goes first. Players take turns placing river tiles. When the stack is exhausted, switch to normal tiles.

  • River tiles must extend the river and may not fold back on themselves.
  • Two consecutive same-direction curves are not allowed.
You may deploy a meeple after placing a river tile, but not on the river itself.
🌸 The Abbot Mini expansion ▾
Contents

1 Abbot figure per player (larger than a meeple).

Deploying the Abbot

When placing a monastery or garden tile, you may deploy your Abbot there. Regular meeples cannot be placed on gardens.

Recalling the Abbot

On your turn, instead of deploying a new meeple, you may recall your Abbot and score it immediately — no need to wait for the feature to complete.

Scoring
Abbot Scoring (same as Monastery)
Fully surrounded by 8 tiles9 pts
End of game (itself + each surrounding tile)1 pt each
🏰 Inns & Cathedrals + Big Meeple +~15 min ▾
Contents

18 new tiles (6 Inn-by-the-Lake, 2 Cathedral); 1 Big Meeple per player.

Inn by the Lake

When a road with an Inn completes, score 2 pts per tile (instead of 1).

If the road is incomplete at game end, it scores 0 pts regardless of the Inn.
Cathedral

When a city with a Cathedral completes, score 3 pts per tile and per shield (instead of 2).

If the city is incomplete at game end, it scores 0 pts regardless of the Cathedral.
Big Meeple

Deployed like a normal meeple but counts as 2 meeples for majority. Returns after scoring.

As a farmer, the Big Meeple counts as two farmers when calculating field majorities.
🐷 Traders & Builders +~15 min ▾
Contents

24 new tiles, 20 trade goods tokens (Wine ×9, Grain ×6, Cloth ×5), 6 Builders, 6 Pigs.

Builder

How to Deploy

May only be placed on a city or road of the tile you just placed, where you already have at least one meeple.

Extra Turn Effect

If you place a tile that expands the city or extends the road where your Builder sits, you immediately take an extra turn: draw and place a second tile, optionally deploy, and score.

  • No second extra turn (even if the second tile also extends the feature).
  • Can trigger every turn as long as the feature is incomplete.
  • One road/city can hold multiple Builders.

Recalling the Builder

Return the Builder when the feature no longer has any of your other meeples. The Builder does not count toward majority.

The Builder is a special figure, not a regular meeple.
Pig

How to Deploy

May only be placed on a field of the tile you just placed, where you already have at least one farmer.

Effect

Farmers in that field score 4 pts per completed city (instead of 3).

The Pig does not count toward farmer majority — it only boosts your scoring rate. Your Pig still gives you more points even in a shared field.
Trade Goods

Claiming Goods

When a city with trade goods completes: ① Score normally. ② The player who placed the completing tile takes all goods of that type, even without a meeple there. Only one player claims goods per completion.

End-Game Scoring

Most tokens of each type (Wine, Grain, Cloth) → 10 pts. Ties each receive 10 pts.

✈ Flying Machines +~10 min ▾
Contents

8 Flying Machine tiles, 1 custom die (faces: 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3).

When Triggered

When you draw a Flying Machine tile, place it normally. Then choose:
A) Deploy a meeple normally to this tile.
B) Use the Flying Machine to send a meeple to another tile.

Landing Rules

Choose a direction (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal), then roll the die for distance (1–3 tiles in a straight line).

  • Must land on an incomplete feature (city, road, or monastery).
  • May land on a feature already occupied by another player's meeple.
  • Cannot land on fields (even unoccupied ones).
  • Cannot land on an empty space (no tile).

If no valid landing spot exists, the meeple is returned and no figure may be deployed this turn.

If multiple valid landing spots exist, you choose where and which role.
📨 Messengers +~15 min ▾
Contents

8 Messenger cards (double-sided), 6 Envoy figures (matching player colors). Each player has one Envoy and one scoring meeple on the scoreboard (together: "scoring figures").

Using Messenger Cards

After each scoring event, move one scoring figure forward. If it lands on a dark space, either use the card's ability or gain 2 pts. Then place the card face-down at the bottom of the deck.

Card Abilities
  • Card 1: Score your lowest-value city as if it were end of game. Meeples stay in place.
  • Card 2: Score your lowest-value road. Same as Card 1 but for roads.
  • Card 3: Score your lowest-value monastery. Same as Card 1 but for monasteries.
  • Card 4: Take an extra turn — draw and place another tile, optionally deploy, and score.
  • Card 5: 2 pts per shield in any city where you have a meeple.
  • Card 6: 2 pts per knight (meeple in a city) you have on the board.
  • Card 7: 2 pts per farmer you have on the board.
  • Card 8: Move one scoring figure to match the other. Choose a feature with at least one of your meeples, score it alone (end-of-game method), then return 1 meeple.
Only one scoring figure may be moved per scoring event. If a card's score lands on another dark space, you may draw another card — chains are unlimited.
End of Game

Before final scoring, each player combines Envoy and meeple scores and advances their marker, then removes the Envoy. No cards are drawn during final scoring.

With Inns & Cathedrals: Cards 1/2 give 0 pts for incomplete Inn/Cathedral features; Cards 6–7 count the Big Meeple as only 1 knight/farmer.

⛵ Ferries +~15 min ▾
Contents

8 lake tiles (including 1 starting tile), 8 wooden ferries.

Placing a Ferry

After optionally deploying a meeple to a road, you must take a ferry and place it on the lake, connecting two roads on the tile. Mandatory regardless of whether you deployed a meeple. The two connected roads are treated as one continuous road.

If you deploy to a road, you must choose one of the roads on that tile.
Moving a Ferry (later turns)

If you place a tile extending at least one road connected by a ferry, you may move that ferry. It must still connect two roads on its tile. If multiple ferries are on the extended road, only the closest one may be moved.

Special case: if your tile extends multiple ferried roads, you may move the closest ferry on each, in any order.
Expansion Interactions

Inns & Cathedrals: A ferried road scores 1 pt per tile if no Inn is present.
Traders & Builders: A Builder's road extended solely by a ferry move does not trigger an extra turn.

🥇 Gold Mines +~10 min ▾
Contents

8 gold mine tiles, 1 scoring chart, 16 wooden gold nuggets.

When Triggered

When you draw a gold mine tile, place it normally, then place 1 gold nugget on that tile and on each orthogonally or diagonally adjacent tile. Multiple nuggets may stack on a single tile.

Claiming Gold

When a feature completes, the player with the most meeples on that feature claims all gold nuggets on its tiles.

Gold Nugget Scoring (at game end)
1–3 nuggets1 pt each
4–6 nuggets2 pts each
7–9 nuggets3 pts each
10+ nuggets4 pts each
Farmers cannot claim gold. Nuggets remaining on tiles at game end are returned to the box.
Tied Majority

In a tie, starting with the active player and going clockwise, each tied player takes one nugget at a time until all are distributed.

🧙 Mage & Witch +~10 min ▾
Contents

8 magic tiles, 1 Mage (purple), 1 Witch (orange). Both are shared figures belonging to no player.

Moving the Mage or Witch

When you draw a magic tile, place it normally. Then, before deploying a meeple, you must move either the Mage or the Witch to any incomplete road or city. The two figures cannot occupy the same road or city.

This step happens before scoring — so you can move one onto a feature that was just completed, affecting the result.

If no placement is possible (no incomplete roads or cities), remove one figure and return it to the supply.
Scoring Effects
  • Mage: +1 pt per tile in the feature.
  • Witch: The feature's total score is halved (round up).

After scoring, remove the figure from that feature and return it to the supply.

End of Game

The Mage and Witch still apply their effects at final scoring, even to incomplete features.

With Inns & Cathedrals: an incomplete Inn/Cathedral feature scores 0; the Mage still adds 1 pt per tile (but shields still score 0).
🦹 Robbers +~10 min ▾
Contents

8 Robber tiles, 6 Robber figures (one per player color).

Placing a Robber

When you draw a Robber tile, place it normally. Then, before deploying a meeple, you may place your Robber on the scoreboard on the same space as another player's scoring marker. Other players clockwise may also place their Robbers.

Stealing

Whenever a player's scoring marker advances, if your Robber is on the same space, you receive half their score (rounded up). Your Robber returns to your supply. The victim does not lose any points.

Other Rules
  • No Robber-on-Robber chains — if a player moves their marker due to being robbed, other Robbers on that new space cannot steal again.
  • A Robber cannot pass on a theft in hopes of a bigger score later.
  • You cannot rob yourself.
End of Game

Any Robber still on the scoreboard scores its owner 3 pts.

With Messengers: if a stolen score lands your marker on a dark space, you may draw a Messenger card.
🌾 Crop Circles +~10 min ▾
Contents

6 Crop Circle tiles. The crop circle itself is a road endpoint and field boundary — meeples may not be placed on the crop circle symbol.

When Triggered

When you draw a Crop Circle tile, complete your normal turn first (① place tile → ② deploy meeple → ③ score), then execute the Crop Circle effect.

The Effect

You choose one of the two effects. Then starting with the player to your left, each player clockwise executes the effect:

  • Effect A (may): You may deploy one meeple of the matching type directly alongside an existing meeple of the same type.
  • Effect B (must): You must recall one meeple of the matching type from the board.
Which Meeple Type
  • Pitchfork crop circle → Farmers
  • Hammer crop circle → Thieves (meeples on roads)
  • Shield crop circle → Knights (meeples in cities)
Under Effect A, only the matching type may be deployed. Players with no matching meeple on the board are skipped. The active player executes the effect last.
🐉 The Princess & The Dragon +~40 min ▾
Contents

30 new tiles (Volcano, Dragon, Princess, Magic Gate symbols), 1 Dragon figure, 1 Fairy figure.

Volcano Tile

Place normally. You cannot deploy a meeple, but you may move the Fairy. Then move the Dragon to this volcano. No rampage is triggered. End your turn.

The Dragon does not exist until the first Volcano appears. If a Dragon tile is drawn first, set it aside face-up and redraw. Once the Volcano appears, shuffle all set-aside Dragon tiles back in.
Dragon Tile (Rampage)

Place normally and optionally deploy a meeple. Then the Dragon rampages.

Rampage Sequence

Starting with the active player, each player clockwise moves the Dragon one step (horizontally or vertically), for 6 total steps (unless blocked).

  • Cannot visit the same tile twice in one rampage.
  • Cannot enter a tile occupied by the Fairy.
  • Any meeple or special figure on a tile the Dragon visits is eaten and returned to its owner.
What the Dragon Eats
Regular meeples, Abbot, Big MeepleEaten
Builder & Pig, Mage & WitchEaten
The FairySafe
Meeples in Castles or Count's CitySafe
Dead end: if the Dragon cannot legally move before completing 6 steps, the rampage ends immediately.
Princess Tile

Place normally. If the tile connects to a city with a knight, you may remove one knight and return it to its owner. If you do, you may not deploy a meeple this turn.

Magic Gate Tile

After placing a Magic Gate tile, you may deploy a meeple to any unoccupied, incomplete feature anywhere on the board — not just the tile you placed.

Normal deployment rules still apply: cannot target occupied or completed features.
The Fairy
  • Move the Fairy: Instead of deploying a meeple, move the Fairy next to any of your meeples (including farmers).
  • +1 pt per turn: At the start of each of your turns, if the Fairy is next to one of your meeples, gain 1 pt.
  • +3 pts on scoring: When the feature next to the Fairy scores, you gain an extra 3 pts. After scoring, your meeple returns but the Fairy stays.
🌉 Bridges, Castles & Bazaars +~40 min ▾
Contents

12 new tiles (8 with Bazaar symbol), 12 Castle tiles, 12 Bridges. 2–4 players: 3 bridges + 3 castle tiles each. 5–6 players: 2 each.

Bridges

Placing a Bridge

After placing a tile, you may build one bridge on the tile you just placed or any orthogonally adjacent tile. Both bridge ends must rest on fields. A bridge acts as a road crossing a tile.

If both ends rest on fields with no connecting road, the bridge is a complete standalone road — it scores 1 pt immediately.

  • A bridge counts as one tile's worth of road but is neither a road start nor end.
  • Bridges may connect to other bridges; only one bridge per tile.
  • A bridge may not be placed on a tile with another player's meeple or game piece.
Castles

Converting a Small City

When you complete a small city (exactly 2 tiles) and you have a meeple there, you may: score 4 pts normally; or place a Castle tile on it and leave the meeple there to score later.

Scoring a Castle

A Castle's neighborhood is the 2 castle tiles plus the 2 tiles to the left and right — 6 tiles total. The Castle scores points equal to the first feature that completes within its neighborhood.

  • You cannot pass — the first completion triggers the Castle, regardless of score.
  • If multiple features complete simultaneously, you choose which triggers the Castle.
  • Castles within each other's neighborhoods all score the same amount simultaneously.
  • If no neighborhood feature completes before game end, the Castle scores 0 pts and the meeple is lost.
At final scoring, farmers score 4 pts per Castle (vs. 3 pts per normal city).
Bazaars

When you draw a Bazaar tile, complete your normal turn first, then run the auction. Skip if fewer tiles remain than there are players.

Auction Procedure

Flip tiles equal to the player count from the top of the stack. Starting left, each player selects a tile and opens bidding at any amount ≥ 0. Others may raise or pass. The selector must then:

  • Buy — pay the highest bid (deduct from score), or
  • Sell — give the tile to the highest bidder and receive the bid amount.

The last remaining tile goes to the player who hasn't bought one yet, for free. Scores can go below 0.

No chain reactions: a Bazaar tile drawn during an auction does not trigger a new auction until the current one resolves.

Variant (no bidding): Reveal tiles equal to the player count. Starting left, each player selects one tile and immediately plays a full normal turn with it.
