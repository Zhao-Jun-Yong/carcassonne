🌱Beginner's GuideFirst time? Start here▾
Each Turn in Three Steps
  • ① Draw & Place: Draw a tile and place it next to existing tiles. All touching edges must match terrain.
  • ② Deploy (optional): Place one of your meeples on a feature of the tile you just placed, provided that feature is not already occupied.
  • ③ Score: If a feature was completed by your placement, score it immediately and return your meeple.
Common Beginner Mistakes
  • Running out of meeples? Normal. Meeples are limited — the strategy is timing your placements to get them back.
  • Farmers never come back? Correct. Farmers (laid flat) stay on the board all game and only score at the end.
  • How do shields score? When a city completes: +2 pts each. At end of game (incomplete): +1 pt each.
  • Can I take over someone's feature? No. But if tiles connect two features, they merge — whoever has more meeples wins; ties share the full score.
  • When does a monastery complete? When all 8 surrounding spaces are filled — 9 pts total.
Meeple Role Names
  • On a road → Thief
  • In a city → Knight
  • On a monastery → Monk
  • Lying flat on a field → Farmer

These role names appear in expansion rules such as Crop Circles and the Dragon's menu.

⚖Referee's Handbook: Disputes & Cross-Expansion RulesSettle arguments here▾
Gold Mines
Q: If a gold mine tile completes a feature on the same turn, can gold be claimed immediately?
Yes. Correct order: ① Place tile → ② Place gold → ③ Score and distribute gold. Gold is placed before scoring, so it can be claimed that same turn.
Q: Can farmers claim gold nuggets?
No. Farmers cannot receive gold at any point — during the game or at final scoring.
Inns & Cathedrals
Q: Can I place a cathedral in someone else's city?
Yes. It's legal sabotage — if they fail to complete that city, they score 0. Inns can similarly be placed on opponents' roads.
Q: If a road or city with an inn/cathedral is incomplete at game end, do they still score?
No — 0 pts. Worse than a normal incomplete feature, which at least scores 1 pt per tile.
Q: Does the Big Meeple count as two meeples for majority?
Yes — counts as 2 meeples when determining majority.
Traders & Builders
Q: Does the Dragon eat the Builder and the Pig?
Yes. Both appear on the Dragon's menu in the Big Box rulebook. They are returned to their owner when eaten.
Q: If the Builder is eaten, can the extra turn still be taken?
No. The Builder is not on the board, so the extra turn condition is not met.
Q: If the Pig is eaten, how do farmers score?
Back to 3 pts per city. The Pig's bonus is lost.
Q: If a Crop Circle tile is drawn during an extra turn, when does the Crop Circle effect trigger?
Complete the entire extra turn first (place tile, deploy meeple, score), then execute the Crop Circle effect.
The Princess & The Dragon
Q: Does the Fairy's 1-pt bonus apply at the start of a Builder extra turn?
No. The Fairy's 1 pt only triggers at the start of a normal turn.
Q: Can the Fairy protect meeples from the Princess tile?
No. The Fairy only protects against the Dragon. Knights displaced by the Princess cannot receive the Fairy's 3-pt bonus either.
Q: Can the Magic Gate deploy a meeple to an already-occupied feature?
No. The Magic Gate follows normal deployment rules — cannot target occupied or completed features.
Q: Can the Dragon eat farmers in fields?
Yes. The Dragon eats all meeples and special figures on any tile it passes through, including farmers.
Q: If the Dragon enters a tile whose feature was already scored but meeples remain, are they eaten?
Yes. The Dragon does not distinguish completed from incomplete features.
Mage & Witch × Princess & Dragon
Q: Does the Dragon eat the Mage or the Witch?
Yes. Both are on the Dragon's menu per the official FAQ. They are returned to the supply when eaten.
Q: After placing a magic tile, the Mage and Witch end up on the same feature — what happens?
The player who placed the tile must immediately move one of them, before scoring.
Q: If there are no meeples on the feature when it scores, do the Mage/Witch effects still apply?
Yes. The Mage still adds 1 pt per tile; the Witch still halves the total (round up). The Mage and Witch cannot be on the same feature simultaneously.
Crop Circles
Q: Effect B forces a recall — what if a player has no matching meeple type on the board?
They are skipped.
Q: Can a player choose not to deploy under Effect A?
Yes. Effect A says "may" — it is optional.
Q: Under Effect A, can a meeple be placed on a feature already occupied by an opponent?
Yes, but only next to your own meeple already there. This follows normal shared-ownership rules.
Ferries
Q: If a Builder's road is extended solely because a ferry moved, does that trigger an extra turn?
No. Extra turns only trigger when a player actually places a new tile.
Q: If a ferry move closes both ends of a road, does it score immediately?
Yes — score immediately. Any event that closes both ends of a road triggers immediate scoring.
Robbers
Q: If a Robber moves due to theft and lands on another scoring marker, does it steal again?
No. No chain robberies — the Robber stops after one theft per trigger.
Q: If multiple Robbers are on the same space, do they all steal?
Yes — each triggers independently. Each Robber's owner receives half the victim's score (rounded up).
Q: Can a Robber steal points triggered by a Messenger card?
Yes. Any scoring action that moves a scoring marker can be stolen from.
Bridges, Castles & Bazaars
Q: How much does a Castle score for a farmer?
4 pts per Castle (vs. 3 pts for a normal completed city).
Q: Can the Dragon enter a tile occupied by a Castle?
No. Meeples in Castles are protected, equivalent to the Count's city.
Q: Can bazaar bidding push a score below 0?
Yes. Negative scores are possible and valid.
Flying Machines
Q: Can a meeple land on an incomplete feature already occupied by an opponent?
Yes. The Flying Machine can land on any incomplete road, city, or monastery regardless of existing occupants. Fields and completed features are prohibited.
Q: What if the dice result points to a space with no tile?
The meeple is returned. No meeple or special figure may be deployed that turn.
Messengers
Q: If multiple features are scored in one turn, can different scoring figures be moved for each?
Yes. Each separate scoring event can use a different figure, but each individual scoring event must be moved by a single figure.
Q: Can Messenger cards chain indefinitely?
Yes — unlimited chains. Each time a scoring figure lands on a dark space, another card may be drawn.
📜Base GameCore rules▾
Overview

2–6 players, 72 land tiles, 7 meeples each. One starting tile placed face-up to begin.

Three Steps Per Turn

① Place a Tile

Draw 1 tile and place it adjacent to existing tiles. All touching edges must match. In the rare case it cannot be placed, discard it and draw again.

② Deploy a Meeple (optional)

Place 1 meeple on a feature of the tile you just placed. You may not deploy to a feature already occupied by any meeple (including your own).

③ Score

If the tile you placed completed any feature, score it immediately and return the meeple(s).

Scoring
During the Game (feature completed)
Road (per tile)1 pt
City (per tile)2 pts
Shield in city (each)+2 pts
Monastery (all 8 surrounding filled)9 pts
End of Game (incomplete features)
Incomplete road (per tile)1 pt
Incomplete city (per tile)1 pt
Shield in incomplete city (each)+1 pt
Incomplete monastery (itself + each surrounding tile)1 pt each
Farmer (per adjacent completed city)3 pts
Shared Ownership

If a feature is shared, the player with the most meeples scores all points. Ties: everyone with the most scores in full.

Farmers

Farmers lie flat on fields and never return during the game. They score only at game end: 3 pts per completed city adjacent to their field.

Shared field rules apply: the player(s) with the most farmers in a field score all points from that field.
Scoreboard

When reaching or passing 50/100, flip your scoring token or take a 50/100 point tile to track your total.

🌊The RiverMini expansion▾
Contents

12 river tiles (darker backs), including the Spring and Lake tiles. These replace the starting tile.

Setup

Find the Spring and Lake tiles. Shuffle the remaining 10 river tiles face-down into a stack, place the Spring on top and the Lake at the bottom.

Play

The player who places the Spring goes first. Players take turns drawing and placing river tiles. When the river stack is exhausted, switch to normal tiles.

  • River tiles must extend the river and may not fold back on themselves.
  • Two consecutive left-turn or right-turn tiles are not allowed.
You may deploy a meeple after placing a river tile, but not on the river itself.
🌸The AbbotMini expansion▾
Contents

1 Abbot figure per player (larger than a meeple).

Deploying the Abbot

When placing a monastery or garden tile, you may deploy your Abbot. The Abbot can only go on monasteries or gardens; regular meeples cannot be placed on gardens.

Recalling the Abbot

On your turn, instead of deploying a new meeple, you may recall your Abbot and score it immediately — no need to wait for the feature to complete.

Scoring
Abbot Scoring (same as Monastery)
Fully surrounded by 8 tiles9 pts
End of game (itself + each surrounding tile)1 pt each
🏰Inns & Cathedrals + Big Meeple+~15 min▾
Contents

18 new tiles (6 Inn-by-the-Lake tiles, 2 Cathedral tiles); 1 Big Meeple per player.

Inn by the Lake

When a road with an Inn completes, score 2 pts per tile (instead of 1).

If the road is incomplete at game end, it scores 0 pts regardless of the Inn.
Cathedral

When a city with a Cathedral completes, score 3 pts per tile and per shield (instead of 2).

If the city is incomplete at game end, it scores 0 pts regardless of the Cathedral.
Big Meeple

Deployed like a normal meeple but counts as 2 meeples for majority. Returns after scoring.

If used as a farmer, the Big Meeple counts as two farmers when calculating field majorities.
🐷Traders & Builders+~15 min▾
Contents

24 new tiles, 20 trade goods tokens (Wine ×9, Grain ×6, Cloth ×5), 6 Builders, 6 Pigs.

Builder

How to Deploy

May only be placed on a city or road of the tile you just placed, where you already have at least one meeple.

Extra Turn Effect

In future turns, if you place a tile that expands the city or extends the road where your Builder sits, you immediately take an extra turn: draw and place a second tile, optionally deploy a meeple, and score.

  • No second extra turn (even if the second tile also extends the feature).
  • Can trigger every turn as long as the feature is incomplete.
  • One road/city can hold multiple Builders.

Recalling the Builder

Return the Builder when the feature no longer has any of your other meeples. The Builder does not count toward majority.

The Builder is a special figure, not a regular meeple.
Pig

How to Deploy

May only be placed on a field of the tile you just placed, where you already have at least one farmer.

Effect

Farmers in that field score 4 pts per completed city (instead of 3).

The Pig does not count toward farmer majority — it only boosts your scoring rate. If you share a field with an opponent, your Pig still gives you more points.
Trade Goods

Claiming Goods

When a city with trade goods completes: ① Score normally. ② The player who placed the completing tile takes all goods matching that city's type, even without a meeple there. Only one player claims goods per completion.

End-Game Scoring

Most tokens of each type (Wine, Grain, Cloth) → 10 pts. Ties each receive 10 pts.

✈Flying Machines+~10 min▾
Contents

8 Flying Machine tiles, 1 custom die (faces: 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3).

When Triggered

When you draw a Flying Machine tile, place it normally. Then choose:
A) Deploy a meeple normally to a road, city, or field on that tile.
B) Use the Flying Machine to send a meeple to another tile.

Landing Rules

Choose a direction (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal), then roll the die to determine distance (1–3 tiles in a straight line).

  • Must land on an incomplete feature (city, road, or monastery).
  • May land on a feature already occupied by another player's meeple.
  • Cannot land on fields (even unoccupied ones).
  • Cannot land on an empty space (no tile).

If the target tile has no valid incomplete non-field feature, or there is no tile there, the meeple is returned and no meeple or special figure may be deployed this turn.

If multiple valid landing spots exist, you choose where and which role.
📨Messengers+~15 min▾
Contents

8 Messenger cards (double-sided), 6 Envoy figures (matching player colors, used as scoring figures). Each player has one Envoy and one scoring meeple on the scoreboard (together: "scoring figures").

Using Messenger Cards

After each scoring event, move one scoring figure forward. If it lands on a dark space, choose to either use the card's ability or gain 2 pts. Then place the card face-down at the bottom of the deck.

Card Abilities
  • Card 1: Score your lowest-value city as if it were end of game. Meeples stay in place.
  • Card 2: Score your lowest-value road. Same as Card 1 but for roads.
  • Card 3: Score your lowest-value monastery. Same as Card 1 but for monasteries.
  • Card 4: Take an extra turn — draw and place another tile, optionally deploy a meeple, and score.
  • Card 5: 2 pts per shield in any city where you have a meeple.
  • Card 6: 2 pts per knight (meeple in a city) you have on the board. Knights stay in place.
  • Card 7: 2 pts per farmer you have on the board. Farmers stay in place.
  • Card 8: Move one scoring figure to match the other. Choose a feature with at least one of your meeples, score it alone (end-of-game method), then return 1 meeple from that feature.
Only one scoring figure may be moved per scoring event. If a card's score lands the figure on another dark space, you may draw another card — chains are unlimited.
End of Game

Before final scoring, each player adds their Envoy and meeple scores and advances their marker accordingly, then removes the Envoy. No cards are drawn during final scoring.

With Inns & Cathedrals: Cards 1/2 give 0 pts for incomplete Inn/Cathedral features; Cards 6–7 count the Big Meeple as only 1 knight/farmer.

⛵Ferries+~15 min▾
Contents

8 lake tiles (including 1 starting tile), 8 wooden ferries.

Placing a Ferry

After optionally deploying a meeple to a road, you must take a ferry from the supply and place it on the lake, connecting two roads on the tile. This is mandatory regardless of whether you deployed a meeple. The two connected roads are treated as one continuous road; the lake serves as the endpoint of any unconnected road.

If you deploy to a road, you must choose one of the three or four roads on that tile.
Moving a Ferry (later turns)

If you place a tile that extends at least one road connected by a ferry, you may (but are not required to) move that ferry. After moving, the ferry must still connect two roads on its tile. If multiple ferries are on the extended road, only the one closest to the new tile may be moved.

Special case: if your tile extends multiple ferried roads, you may move the closest ferry on each of those roads, in any order you choose.
Expansion Interactions

Inns & Cathedrals: A road connected by ferry scores 1 pt per tile if no Inn is present.
Traders & Builders: A Builder's road extended solely by a ferry move does not trigger an extra turn.

🥇Gold Mines+~10 min▾
Contents

8 gold mine tiles, 1 scoring chart, 16 wooden gold nuggets.

When Triggered

When you draw a gold mine tile, place it normally, then place 1 gold nugget on that tile and on each tile orthogonally or diagonally adjacent to it. Multiple nuggets may stack on a single tile.

Claiming Gold

When a feature completes, the player with the most meeples on that feature claims all gold nuggets on its tiles.

Gold Nugget Scoring (converted at game end)
1–3 nuggets1 pt each
4–6 nuggets2 pts each
7–9 nuggets3 pts each
10+ nuggets4 pts each
Farmers cannot claim gold. Any nuggets remaining on tiles at game end are returned to the box.
Tied Majority

In a tie, starting with the player whose turn it is and going clockwise, each tied player takes one nugget at a time until all are distributed.

🧙Mage & Witch+~10 min▾
Contents

8 magic tiles, 1 Mage (purple), 1 Witch (orange). Both are shared figures belonging to no player.

Moving the Mage or Witch

When you draw a magic tile, place it normally. Then, before deploying a meeple, you must move either the Mage or the Witch to any incomplete road or city. The two figures cannot occupy the same road or city.

This step happens before scoring. If placing your tile completes a feature, you can move the Mage or Witch onto it before scoring — affecting the result.

If no placement is possible (no incomplete roads or cities), remove one figure and return it to the supply.
Scoring Effects
  • Mage: +1 pt per tile in the feature.
  • Witch: The feature's total score is halved (round up).

After scoring, remove the figure from that feature and return it to the supply.

End of Game

The Mage and Witch still apply their effects at final scoring (even to incomplete features).

With Inns & Cathedrals: an incomplete Inn/Cathedral feature scores 0; if the Mage is present, it still adds 1 pt per tile (but shields still score 0).
🦹Robbers+~10 min▾
Contents

8 Robber tiles, 6 Robber figures (one per player color).

Placing a Robber

When you draw a Robber tile, place it normally. Then, before deploying a meeple, you may place your Robber on the scoreboard on the same space as another player's scoring marker. Then each other player clockwise may also place their Robber.

Stealing

Whenever a player's scoring marker advances due to scoring, if your Robber is on the same space, you receive half their score (rounded up — e.g., opponent scores 5, you get 3). Your Robber then returns to your supply. The victim does not lose any points.

Other Rules
  • If a player moves their marker to record points they received from being robbed, and your Robber is on that new space, you may not steal again from that move (no Robber-on-Robber chains).
  • A Robber cannot pass on an opportunity to steal in hopes of a bigger score later.
  • You cannot rob yourself.
End of Game

Any Robber still on the scoreboard at game end scores its owner 3 pts.

With Messengers: if a stolen score lands your marker on a dark space, you may draw a Messenger card.
🌾Crop Circles+~10 min▾
Contents

6 Crop Circle tiles. The crop circle itself is a road endpoint and field boundary — meeples may not be placed on the crop circle symbol.

When Triggered

When you draw a Crop Circle tile, complete your normal turn first (① place tile → ② deploy meeple → ③ score), then execute the Crop Circle effect.

The Effect

You choose one of the two effects below. Then starting with the player to your left, each player clockwise executes the effect in turn:

  • Effect A (may): You may deploy one meeple of the matching type directly alongside an existing meeple of the same type on the board.
  • Effect B (must): You must recall one meeple of the matching type from the board.
Which Meeple Type
  • Pitchfork crop circle → Farmers (meeples in fields).
  • Hammer crop circle → Thieves (meeples on roads).
  • Shield crop circle → Knights (meeples in cities).
Under Effect A, only the matching meeple type may be deployed. If a player has no matching meeple on the board, they are skipped. The active player executes the effect last.
🐉The Princess & The Dragon+~40 min▾
Contents

30 new tiles (Volcano, Dragon, Princess, Magic Gate symbols), 1 Dragon figure, 1 Fairy figure.

🌋 Volcano Tile

Place normally. You cannot deploy a meeple, but you may move the Fairy. Then move the Dragon to this volcano. If the Dragon is already on the board, it simply moves to the new volcano — no rampage is triggered. End your turn.

The Dragon does not exist until the first Volcano tile appears. If a Dragon tile is drawn before a Volcano, set it aside face-up and draw again. Once the Volcano appears, shuffle all set-aside Dragon tiles back into the stack.
🔥 Dragon Tile (Rampage)

Place normally and optionally deploy a meeple. Then the Dragon rampages.

Rampage Sequence

Starting with the player whose turn it is, each player clockwise moves the Dragon one step (horizontally or vertically), for 6 total steps (unless blocked).

  • The Dragon cannot visit the same tile twice in one rampage.
  • The Dragon cannot enter a tile occupied by the Fairy.
  • Any meeple or special figure on a tile the Dragon passes through or stops on is eaten and returned to its owner.

👑 Princess Tile

Place normally. If the tile connects to a city that already has a knight, you may remove one knight from that city and return it to its owner.

If you remove a knight, you may not deploy a meeple this turn. If the city has no knights (or you choose not to remove one), deploy normally.

🚪 Magic Gate Tile

After placing a Magic Gate tile, you may deploy a meeple to this tile or any other tile on the board, following all normal deployment rules (no occupied features, no completed features). If no legal placement exists anywhere, no meeple may be deployed this turn.

🧚 The Fairy

The Fairy is a shared figure belonging to no player.

Moving the Fairy

Moving the Fairy replaces deploying a meeple — choose one or the other. The Fairy may be placed next to any of your meeples, including farmers in fields.

Fairy Effects (while next to your meeple)

  • ① 1 pt at turn start: At the start of your normal turn (not a Builder extra turn), gain 1 pt if the Fairy is next to one of your meeples.
  • ② Dragon protection: The Dragon cannot enter a tile occupied by the Fairy, protecting all meeples there.
  • ③ +3 pts on scoring: When the feature next to the Fairy scores, you gain an extra 3 pts regardless of whether your meeple won. After scoring, your meeple returns but the Fairy stays.
🌉Bridges, Castles & Bazaars+~40 min▾
Contents

12 new tiles (8 with Bazaar symbol), 12 Castle tiles, 12 Bridges. 2–4 players: 3 bridges + 3 castle tiles each. 5–6 players: 2 each.

Bridges

Placing a Bridge

After placing a tile, you may build one bridge. A bridge acts as a road crossing from one side of a tile to the other. It may be built on the tile you just placed or any orthogonally adjacent tile. Both bridge ends must rest on fields (not cities, roads, or rivers).

A bridge does not need to connect to existing roads. If both ends rest on fields with no connecting road, the bridge is a complete standalone road — it scores 1 pt immediately when placed.

  • A bridge counts as one tile's worth of road but is neither a road start nor end.
  • Bridges may connect to other bridges; only one bridge per tile.
  • A bridge may be placed on a tile occupied by a monastery.
  • A bridge may not be placed on a tile containing another player's meeple or game piece.
You may use a bridge to connect a road to a field on the same turn as placement.
Castles

Converting a Pennant City

When you complete a small city (exactly 2 tiles), the player occupying it may choose: score 4 pts and return meeples as normal; or place a Castle tile on it and leave the meeple there to score later.

You must have a meeple in the city to build a Castle. Which tile the meeple was placed on does not affect eligibility.

Scoring a Castle

A Castle's "neighborhood" is the 2 castle tiles plus the 2 tiles to the left and 2 tiles to the right — 6 tiles total.

The Castle scores points equal to the first feature that completes anywhere within its neighborhood. The meeple returns, but the Castle tile remains for the rest of the game.

  • You cannot pass — the first completion within the neighborhood triggers the Castle, no matter the score.
  • If multiple features complete simultaneously in the same turn, you may choose which one the Castle scores from.
  • If you own both the Castle and the completing feature, you score both separately.
  • Castles within each other's neighborhoods chain together and all score the same amount simultaneously.
  • If the game ends before any feature in the neighborhood completes, the Castle scores 0 pts and the meeple is lost.
At final scoring, farmers score 4 pts per Castle (vs. 3 pts per normal city).
Bazaars

When you draw a Bazaar tile, complete your normal turn first (place tile → deploy meeple → score), then run the auction.

Auction Procedure

Skip the auction if fewer tiles are available than there are players. Otherwise, flip over tiles equal to the player count from the top of the stack.

Starting with the player to your left, they select a tile and open bidding at any amount ≥ 0 pts (no negative bids). Each other player clockwise may raise the bid or pass. After all players have had one chance, the player who selected the tile must:

  • Buy the tile — pay the highest bid (deduct from their score), or
  • Sell the tile to the highest bidder — receive the bid amount (add to their score).

If no one outbid the opener (highest bid = 0), they take the tile for free. Scores can go below 0 — this is normal, especially early in the game.

The last remaining tile goes to the player who hasn't bought one yet, for free.

No chain reactions: if a Bazaar tile appears during an auction, it does not trigger a new auction until the current one is fully resolved and that player completes their normal turn.

Variant (no bidding): After your normal turn, reveal tiles equal to the player count. Starting left, each player clockwise selects one tile and immediately plays a full normal turn with it.
